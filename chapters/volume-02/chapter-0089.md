# Chapter 89 — Two Days

They went in on Monday afternoon, the seventeenth of November, the seventy-second day, with a sheet of paper and four lines on it, and they stood in a corridor on the second floor of an Office building on the Meridian for eleven minutes and did not go in, and that was the correct decision and it had been made by nobody.

The third sitting is on Wednesday the nineteenth at ten in the morning in the inquiry room, which is the day after tomorrow, and which is also the near end of the team's own records request, filed by hand at a counter in Cordell on a Wednesday in October and quoted at four to six weeks, and the room had been prepared on the Monday afternoon: the long table out, the microphone at the end of it rather than the middle, eight chairs, a jug of water, a woman in a gray jacket going round the jugs with a cloth.

They were not going to be let in and they were not going to ask. A room that has been set out for a hearing is a room that is being prepared, and a room that is being prepared is not yet a record, and a thing you stand in before it becomes a record is a thing you will remember differently afterwards.

"There is a jug of water in there," Tomas Vale said, "and eight chairs, and one of those chairs is going to have a name card on it on Wednesday morning, and tomorrow somebody is going to decide which one, and none of those five decisions is going to be made by anybody who is on this corridor."

"So we stand here," Joon Park said.

"We stand here for about ten minutes so that we have done something," Elias Rook said, "and then we go and have a coffee, and then we go home, and none of that is tactics. I would like it on the page that it is not tactics. I have watched four competent people in nine weeks make a decision in a corridor and then need it to have been a plan."

---

They went and had a coffee. Then they went back to the third floor and had the conversation they had come to have, in a records room with two chairs and no table, standing up, because there is no table in it and never has been.

"Can I read you the two things the request names," Joon Park said, "since you are the one who is going to have to say on Wednesday why neither of them is here."

"Read them."

"**One: the designation history of sector 4C from the first consolidation in two thousand and thirty-nine, with the intermediate sheets, not the summary.** Two: the holding register for the same store, the page for the eleventh of May two thousand and thirty-nine, and the page either side of it. That is the whole of it. It went in by hand at a counter in another city on the Wednesday of the forty-sixth day, the twenty-second of October, out of a quarter to eleven to the two, at a cost of two dollars sixty, one fare out of my own pocket because the hall agreed to one, and it was quoted at four to six weeks.

"The near end is Wednesday morning at ten o'clock. **The far end is the third of December.**"

"Then neither of them is here," Solano said, "and I can tell you why neither of them is here without asking anybody, and you should know it before Wednesday so that it is not a surprise in a room. **A designation history is a document about a shelf, and a holding register is a document about a shelf, and neither of them has ever been requested by name by the body that built them, because the body that built them is a store and a store does not know what it is for.** They are in that building because that is where things go, and they will be produced on the third of December to a man at a counter, correctly, with a stamp, and nobody will be harmed by the delay and nobody will be comforted by the speed, and in the meantime the inquiry is going to sit in a room on Wednesday morning with two documents outstanding that it could have had in October.

"That is not a scandal. **That is what an institution is, and if you want me to say anything else about it, you are going to have to give me a different question, and there is not one.**"

"Right," Counsel Solano said. "What have you got, and I am asking in that order because the order is the whole of what I am allowed."

Joon Park handed her a sheet of hall paper with four lines on it in his own hand.

> **1. 14 January 2003. A form revision. One line added, being a consent line for a subject unable to give consent.**
> **2. DS-4, Revision C. Office of Continuity, Forms and Returns. The form is in a drawer in a form store and cannot be produced.**
> **3. Requested by: District 9. A unit, in a block. No name on the revision letter.**
> **4. The word the Office uses to report a discrepancy between two records: error.**

Solano read it twice.

"That is a good document," she said, and then she said the rest of it, which is what she does. "And it is not evidence, and it is not about your crew, and I am going to put it in a file as a fact with a date on it and I am not going to rule on any part of it, and I am going to say now, out loud, in a room with four people in it, that this is the same procedure I have applied to everything you have given me since the thirty-fourth day and that I am not going to soften it for you this afternoon.

"And I am going to say the other thing, which is the thing that is going to cost me and I would rather you had it in a corridor than in my report in December.

"**You have brought me a form and I cannot use it, and you know I cannot use it, and you have brought it anyway, and the reason you have brought it is that it is the only thing you have.** And I am going to write in my report that the team placed before the inquiry a form revision of the fourteenth of January two thousand and three, and that the form is a designated-site incident return of this office, and that the line in question is a consent line, and that it was added after a night, and that the revision letter carries no name.

"And then somebody in a room in about four years is going to read that paragraph and ask the obvious question, which is *so whose form, and so whose incident*, and there is no next line. **You have handed me a sentence with a door at the end of it and no room behind the door, and I have to write it down and stop, and I do not know whether I have ever written anything like that before and I have been doing this for sixteen years.**"

She looked up.

"Also: your covering letter of the thirty-seventh day promised that the omission in the equipment list would be corrected as an administrative error. **It has not been corrected.** I have checked the file twice this month and I am telling you that I checked it twice this month. I cannot make it be corrected. I do not have the thing that would let me make it be corrected, and I am not going to tell you that I have gone looking for something clever, because there is nothing clever, there is a form and it does not do that, and in a year it will do it, and I am not going to be here in a year."

Nobody said anything for a while.

"That is four sentences and it is all I have and I would like to go and write three hundred pages." She took the sheet. "I will have this typed by Wednesday morning and it will be in front of the Deputy Director at ten o'clock, and it will have my name at the bottom of it, and the fact that my name is at the bottom of it is the only new thing in this business in ten weeks and I am telling you the price of it: **in four years it will read as though a lawyer found it, and a lawyer cannot be asked what she thinks it means by the people it was about, and that is the correct arrangement and I would like somebody in this corridor to write down that I said *correct arrangement* about the thing that has cost me my month.**"

---

They walked out at about half past four, and they stood on the steps, and the light came off the Meridian at the angle it comes off it in November, which is not far, and Elias Rook said the thing he had been carrying since about ten o'clock in the morning.

"I have got the wrong idea again and I want to say it while I am standing here and not on Wednesday.

"I have spent all week being pleased. Three of us in a records room and a form revision in a drawer and a man in a shop who cannot remember his own night. **And I have been carrying that around like it was a shield, and it is not a shield, and this morning in that corridor I understood exactly what it is: it is a fact about their paperwork.**

"That is all it is. The Office's own paperwork is badly designed in one small place and then correctly designed in a hundred others, and the fact that I have found the badly designed one does not make one single thing I have done since the sixth of September into a good idea. It does not make the corridor right. It does not make the seal right or the classification right or the eleven meters right or the twenty-seven hours and thirty-nine minutes right. It does not make anything I did in September into something a man was entitled to do.

"And I have been hoping — I have to say it out loud, I was **hoping** — that if I could get hold of a piece of their own machinery then the rest of it would get lighter. That is not how any of this works and I have known that since October and I have been behaving as though I did not."

Nobody improved on that for about a minute, which is what it was worth.

---

Mara Okafor said, at about five, and she was the one who said it, and she said it flatly and once.

"You are not going to get a defense out of a form number and I am going to say the thing about the rule while we are all standing on steps, because it is the only day I am going to be able to say it and the hearing is in forty-one hours.

"**Any offer, any boundary, any refusal, and any condition somebody is asked to perform before they give anything goes to the people who pay for it, in the words it was said in, before it is acted on.** Wednesday is an offer. It has been an offer since the thirty-fourth day. Four people are going to be asked to give something they have given every day for ten weeks.

"So it goes to them tonight, in the words it is said in. Not to you, and not to me, and not to Mr. Park. I am telling you that now and I am not going to discuss it with you first, because that is the trick and you have both seen me do it and you know why I do it.

"And nobody inherits me. Which means there is going to be somebody standing in that room on Wednesday who decides to say no to me, out loud, and I am not going to be able to do anything about it, and I have known that since the second week and I have still not worked out what I do about it."

And that was the last of the day, and the four of them got four buses, and Tomas Vale went the long way on purpose, and the room on the second floor was still being set out for Wednesday, and nobody had sat in it, and the water jug was still on the table, and the request that had been filed by hand at a counter in another city on a Wednesday in October was due at its near end in forty-one hours with two documents in it and neither of them in hand.
