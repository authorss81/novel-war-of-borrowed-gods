# Novel Specification

Title: The War of Borrowed Gods
Repository slug: novel-war-of-borrowed-gods
Genre: modern fantasy / temporal progression
Male lead: A disaster responder who borrows power from future selves
Length target: 1000 chapters
Relationship policy: One slow-burn relationship or no romance, decided in the bible phase.
Power rule: Growth must be earned through practice, cost, failure, relationships, and changed decisions. The lead must not become instantly overpowered unless the approved genre premise requires it.

## Premise
A modern disaster responder borrows power from future versions of people, and every borrowed god changes the future that lends the power.

## Ending
He prevents the war without erasing the futures that gave him power.
