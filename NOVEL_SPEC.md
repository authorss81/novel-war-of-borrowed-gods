# Novel Specification

Title: The War of Borrowed Gods
Repository slug: novel-war-of-borrowed-gods
Genre: modern fantasy / temporal progression
Male lead: A disaster responder who borrows power from future selves
Length target: 1000 chapters
Relationship policy: One slow-burn relationship or no romance, decided in the bible phase.
Power rule: Growth must be earned through practice, cost, failure, relationships, and changed decisions. The lead must not become instantly overpowered unless the approved genre premise requires it.

## Premise
A modern disaster responder borrows power from future versions of people, and every borrowed god changes the future that lends the power.

## Ending
He prevents the war without erasing the futures that gave him power.

**What Volume 03 is:** a city pilot uses a future engineer's load-reading gift — **Tomas Vale's own Load Reader, and the one man in the city who could read that ground honestly is barred from being consulted, on a designated site, by his own signature** — to save a district from a planned demolition. **The prediction is accurate. The pilot also chooses which residents stay on the official map, and the choice is made by a form and signed by a man.** A future Mara refuses to lend her hands when the present crew forces the pilot's method, and the refusal is consistent with boundaries she has already stated in this manuscript and is not a random outage. The district survives and several branch survivors return with legal and familial conflicts. **The Choir's first public leader argues that a single chosen future is kinder than this uncertainty, and his argument attracts people who have already lost too much to wait: he is the series' first named political opponent and the volume's antagonist, not a cosmic threat.**
