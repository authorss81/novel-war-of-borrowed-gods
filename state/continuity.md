# Continuity State

## Opening canon

- The story opens in 2042 at Kestrel Station and the Salt Meridian. Elias Rook is 35, a Havenport Rescue Service field technician, and began Volume 01 at **Stage 0: Unborrowed**.
- Elias is competent with ordinary rigging, confined-space entry, thermal imaging, breathing gear, seam safety, and human retreat lines. His central movement is from solitary command toward disclosure, delegation, and bounded promises; he is not a passive vessel or an instant super-rescuer.
- Nell Rook is 27, a rail signal engineer inside Kestrel when the collapse begins. Her direct pre-collapse alarm is separate from the later clipped signal. Mara Okafor, Tomas Vale, Joon Park, Inez Sato, and Sable Arden enter with the roles fixed in the character bible. Elias and Mara are professional partners, not romantic partners, at the opening, at the end of Batch 0001, and at the end of Batch 0002.

## Still Hand lock and aftermath

- The first loan is future Mara’s bounded **Still Hand**: keep one named living body, Tarin Voss, from failing while present Mara repairs him. It cannot heal, resurrect, protect Elias from crushing force, or solve the tunnel. The Chapter 2 offer names the gift in a `Gift name` field, so **Still Hand** may be used freely in later prose.
- Elias disclosed the offer, target, boundary, and return before acceptance. Present Mara consented to ordinary emergency treatment and her own repair work under emergency rules, explicitly not on behalf of future Mara.
- Future Mara’s branch-specific reason is a rescue in which a living person was left behind while a cleaner route was pursued. Her boundary forbids killing, forced treatment, and abandonment. The third maintenance wheel is the concrete automatic return trigger.
- The Still Hand faded when the third wheel stopped. Elias cannot voluntarily release, extend, or renew it. Chapter 9's possible extension was refused by the same future Mara for the same reason, and Chapter 7 produced a deliberate `No answer` to a request to reopen the exchange. **No new loan has been accepted anywhere in Volume 01 so far.**
- Elias has Stage 1: First Witness knowledge. He cannot reopen this loan, summon Mara’s voice, or treat a branch memory as a command.
- Elias loses sensation and fine control in his left hand. A triage medic in Chapter 4 requires him to say out loud when the hand fails so another person can take the job. This is a standing practice and it now appears repeatedly in his own speech; it is a physical limit, not a symbol.

## The local authority structure

- The Cinder Quarter refuge is a physical and civic tool: batteries, lamps, seam glass in a wooden frame, analog maps, and mechanical barriers. It cannot stabilise Kestrel, open a seam, or substitute for a safe re-entry plan.
- The first vote (Chapter 15) returned 31 consent, 9 refusal, 4 abstain, with refusals read aloud. The second vote (Chapter 20) returned 26 consent, 7 refusal, 1 abstention.
- Standing conditions: no borrowed circuit; no resident as an anchor in any sense; any person may withdraw their own card, lamp, and name with no reason and no meeting; the Office gets a power summary and not the residents’ log, lamp cards, or consent cards; a blackout means the team goes dark on its own batteries within ninety seconds and nobody is asked to stay in the dark.
- The authority split is on the hall wall: Mara medical triage and the stop on moving any patient; Tomas structural limits and the stop threshold, written before anybody goes; Joon records, custody, and publication; Inez local power and the right to cut it; residents consent to the space; **Elias physical movement, and no sole command.**
- **The five-name stop plan.** Elias, Tomas, Mara, Joon, and Inez may each call a stop, and so may the hall, by sending anybody out the door. The Chapter 18 instance is written, enforced, and expired; the Chapter 20 listening campaign has a fresh one. The plan carries an expiry date at Elias’s request, so it cannot become a procedure with his name on it.
- **The five-signature promise.** Elias will not go below the west apron alone, and not at all unless four of five agree in the same week; if one says no he does not go, does not seek an overrule, and does not mention that he went. The hall may stop it. Signed by Elias, Mara, Tomas, Joon, and Inez, and copied out by Petar Lisk.
- The 2031 repair pour in the apron slab runs eleven metres north-north-east and terminates six metres short of where the cameras point; a brick lining sits about a metre and a half beyond a gap at seven metres on the bore line.
- Ansel Duarte’s condition, accepted by the team: if the team goes back down, the purpose is **a conversation, not an extraction**, and *almost* is not to be written in any document.
- Mara’s condition, accepted by the team: **no medic on the line.** A medic stays at the top, and if somebody is found, treatment happens where they are found, in their air, at their pace.

## The Office and the ninety-six-hour hold

- Sable Arden’s safety argument is credible and specific: the 2019 Salt Crossing outfall, where an unlicensed crew opened a shaft and six people died; Meridian 2029, eleven dead; a 2031 contractor survey finding a migrating void two hundred and ten metres from the Quarter’s clinic wall; and the site’s designated-seam-site classification pack.
- The **ninety-six-hour hold** accepted in Chapter 19: no seal, no equipment seizure, no site entry, classification provisional and unanswered, in exchange for the survey data entering a sealed Office node and a supervised reading of the originals in the hall. The Office must carry the data with the stop page, the refusal, and Halla Dren’s request attached, or the copy is not the same copy and the hold is void.
- A review board convenes on the fourth day of the hold, which is the Saturday after the hold was accepted on Wednesday afternoon. **Hollis Vane**, the Office archivist, reads the originals in the hall, writes his findings in the room, and has asked Elias whether the family connection to Nell should be recorded in the request. Elias said leave it out, so the request stands on the structure.
- The Office’s answer in the **1800 civic digest** is public, courteous, and cites the eleven real fatalities behind *one approved outcome*. It is a concrete institutional pressure, not a revealed ideology, and it is not cartoonish.
- **Perry Dunne** asked for the crew to be gone by Friday so the Quarter has a week where nobody from outside tells him what it needs. The date has not moved and is written on the board.
- Elias has never surrendered the original notebook, and the Office has copies only. He refused a controlled re-entry in exchange for the originals in Chapter 14 and offered a supervised reading instead.
