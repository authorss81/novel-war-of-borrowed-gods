# Continuity State

Planning canon is established in the Volume 01 outline and the repaired Batch 0001 cards. No prose chapter has yet changed that canon; these are binding constraints for Chapters 1–10.

## Opening state

- The story opens in 2042 at Kestrel Station and the Salt Meridian. Elias Rook is 35, a Havenport Rescue Service field technician, and begins at **Stage 0: Unborrowed**. He is competent with ordinary rigging, confined-space entry, thermal imaging, breathing gear, and seam warning signs.
- Nell Rook is 27, a rail signal engineer, inside Kestrel when the collapse begins. Her last direct alarm is a present-day transmission. The later clipped signal is a present-day branch-crossed signal, not a future Nell echo and not a pre-collapse recording.
- Mara Okafor, Tomas Vale, Joon Park, Inez Sato, and Sable Arden enter with the roles fixed in the character bible. Elias and Mara are professional partners, not romantic partners at the opening.

## Batch 0001 bookkeeping

- **Group A:** 15 people from Car 3, including Tarin Voss, the Still Hand target, and one mobility-impaired passenger. They move toward the surface in Chapters 2–4.
- **Group B:** one person on the Car 3 manifest, not present in the first physical count and found alive in Chapter 5. This person is not Nadi Bell.
- **Group C:** seven people in the service alcove behind the changed wall, including Nadi Bell and one patient who cannot walk. They are reached in Chapter 9 and are not in Car 3.
- **Group D:** two deeper heat signatures, with unknown group size and condition. They are not rescued in this batch.
- Groups A and B make a paper count of 16 at the refuge; Group C raises it to 23. The Civic Spine’s report that Car 3 carried no passengers contradicts the physical rescue, while Nadi’s group is a separate location.

## Still Hand lock

- The loan is future Mara’s bounded gift: keep one living body from failing while present Mara repairs it. Its sole target in this batch is Tarin Voss. It cannot heal, resurrect, protect Elias from crushing force, or solve the tunnel.
- Chapters 2–6 are one linked operation tied to the active Kestrel seam and the maintenance trolley, not separate carried scenes. The third wheel is the concrete return trigger; when it stops, the loan ends automatically. Elias can refuse to extend or force it, but cannot voluntarily release or renew the gift.
- The repair changes the seam aftermath and produces a service-door route. The door is not directly powered or sustained by the gift. After the return it becomes structurally unusable and drops out of the immediate route index; whether it has shifted, remains unsafe, or is physically absent is not yet proven. The stronger physical-absence proof is reserved for Chapter 25.
- Elias loses sensation and fine control in one hand and has a brief branch-memory afterimage. No supernatural capacity, persistent thread, or second gift remains after Chapter 6.

## Consent and voices

- Present Mara consents to ordinary emergency treatment and her own repair work, not on behalf of future Mara. Elias discloses the offer, boundary, target, and automatic return before accepting. A later disclosure failure concerns incomplete explanation of branch memory, not secret acceptance; the larger present-consent rupture remains reserved for the planned later-volume beat.
- Future Mara’s branch-specific want is to prevent another living person from being left behind. She can refuse or withdraw, and in Chapter 9 she deliberately refuses a second-body extension.
- The unidentified possible-Elias voice is a warning only. It wants contradictory paper and audio evidence preserved, offers no gift or route command, refuses identification, and can stop if Elias treats it as proof of the Returning Hand. It is not Elias Forty-Seven and is not identified as the Returning Hand in Volume 01.
- Chapter 7’s `No answer` is a deliberate refusal, not relay failure. The possible-Elias warning is raw relay audio, not a second interface panel. Keep interface notices rare and concrete.

## Record and handoff clues

- Nadi gives Joon a family contact and a pre-collapse shuttle number. A paper manifest contains the Bell family, but the current digital response list omits the row. The erasure is seeded, not explained.
- Sable’s form contains an equipment-failure code and timestamp earlier than the rescue report. The next batch compares it with the second author code on the pre-collapse route entry.
- The service door is structurally unusable and index-missing after Chapter 6, not proven physically absent. The next batch must not collapse those states.
- The first loan is over by Chapter 7. The prototype refuge in Chapter 10 is a physical and civic tool, not a shared-load circuit.
