# Character State After Chapter 20

## Elias Rook

- **External position:** Havenport Rescue Service field technician. **Stage 1: First Witness.** No active supernatural capacity. He holds the original notebook, a sealed envelope he has not sent, and a public objection with his name at the bottom of it.
- **Physical condition:** Left hand has persistent numbness and unreliable fine control. He announces it out loud almost every time it fails — “Left hand’s gone” — and hands the job over. The exception is the moment on the bore-scope line in Chapter 18, when it opens, the line jumps two metres, and he does not get the words out. That silence is the only time the habit fails and it costs him the line. By Chapter 20 he can do that without arguing, and other people have learned to offer the job before he asks. The hand still fails at the worst possible moment: it opened on the bore-scope line in Chapter 18 and jumped the line two metres, which is a cost, not a symbol.
- **Emotional position:** He has given up the comfort of a plan that is his. The batch shows him handing the drawing to Joon, the tape end to Kellan, the order to nobody, and finally the decision to stop. He is not fixed: he still wants the route to Nell more than he wants anything else, and Chapter 20 has him naming that want aloud as the thing most likely to make him do something the room votes against.
- **Active want:** Understand the 2031 repair pour and the buried node under the west apron; find out who used `MNT-4C-17`; make contact with the person behind the wall without turning contact into extraction; and reach Nell without making her a retrieval target.
- **Working change:** He makes promises small enough to keep and puts other people in the position to break them. The five-signature promise is the proof: no descent alone, four of five must agree in the same week, and the hall can stop it. He asked for the stop plan to carry an expiry date so it could not become a procedure with his name on it. He told a reporter on the record that he does not know which account becomes the official story, and he said it in front of four other people who had already stated their own domains.
- **Important limit:** He cannot summon a future voice, reopen the Still Hand, or make a branch memory prove a route. He asked Halla Dren for nothing and did not ask twice, and he accepted a survivor’s redefinition of the mission over his own.

## Mara Okafor

- **Professional position:** Emergency physician and triage lead. Sole holder of the medical ledger, which the Office reads and never holds. She holds medical triage and the stop on moving any patient.
- **Emotional position:** Disturbed that Elias accepted a capacity carrying her name before fully explaining the branch-memory effect, and still working with him because he corrected the record, accepted a refusal, and let her dictate the full loan entry into a document the Office has countersigned. Their relationship is professional, slow, and built on conduct. Nothing romantic has happened and no future version has promised them anything.
- **Active want:** Make consent, triage, and refusal legible in emergency practice; keep the medical record honest enough that a future person’s refusal cannot be edited into a malfunction; stop the team building a plan that puts a medic on a line where a silence would be misread.
- **Working change:** She dictated the exact loan boundary, return, refusal, and borrower disclosure into a signed medical entry and made the Office sign it. She refused a medic on the listening line and the plan changed because of it. She told Elias “which is why I have four of you” and meant it, and it was not irony.

## Nell Rook

- **Status:** Missing and not extracted. Her present-day, branch-crossed signal has carried the north stair warning, the people-behind-the-wall warning, an east-branch fragment, and — as of Chapter 20 — four words: **the east branch holds.**
- **New technical fact:** Her signal carrier and the 05:17 maintenance route entry share a physical path. The old maintenance route under Kestrel is how her voice arrives. This is a carrier comparison, not a location, and it is the strongest new pressure on Elias in the batch.
- **Active want within the evidence:** Warn Elias off routes that are branches, keep something open, and be heard without being retrieved.
- **Relationship pressure:** Elias’s reply is still unsent and now sits in his notebook marked **NOT FILED. NOT SENT. A PROMISE I HAVE NOT EARNED THE RIGHT TO MAKE YET.** Nobody has received it. He has told a reporter he will not let a private family connection into a public request, so the citizens’ inspection request stands on the structure alone.

## Tomas Vale

- **Professional position:** Senior safety instructor and structural support lead. Holds structural limits and the stop threshold, written before anyone goes, and no longer the only voice allowed to say the word rule.
- **Disclosed history:** The 2034 Kestrel collapse began with the same east-leg draw and the same slab cycle over a buried paired node under the west apron, written off in 2035 with his signature second on the page. He sat on the 2029 panel that wrote the one-voice safety rule. He signed the reroute that left Samuel Rook holding a frame. He has been giving Elias the softer version of that for eight years and has admitted it.
- **Undisclosed:** A notebook of admissions he says he has not yet earned the right to make, stored with the 2034 report. Nobody has read it.
- **Emotional position:** Wry, ashamed, and still useful. He lost the authority to be the only voice for safety and kept the responsibility, and he said so plainly. He has accepted that a number written down before the work is worth more than a senior man’s judgement in the moment.
- **Active want:** Keep a survey survivable and shareable, and see the rule he wrote dismantled without anybody replacing it with a worse rule.

## Joon Park

- **Professional position:** Dispatcher and record keeper. Sole holder of the raw audio, the manifest, the measurements, the eleven witness statements, and the Bell file, in five or six buildings, none of them his alone. Holds records, custody, and publication, and is the only person authorised by Nadi Bell to ask anything of her sister.
- **Emotional position:** He has become the person who says no on the record. He refused the terminal’s deletion suggestion, refused to leave the code on a wall without “holder unknown,” and refused Halla Dren’s request to leave a gap — in the same hand on the same page as the request, so that nobody can find one without the other.
- **Active want:** Keep a record that can be checked by the people in it, and keep a refusal as legible as an acceptance.

## Inez Sato

- **Professional position:** Cinder Quarter Electrical Cooperative organizer and local power steward. Sole holder of the residents’ lock-box, the power budget, the consent cards, and the right to cut the feeder. Holds local power and the right to cut it.
- **Emotional position:** Firmly allied, on conditions, and unimpressed. She refused to let Elias brief the meeting, made the crew sign cards individually, read every refusal aloud, and wrote the cost of the ninety-six-hour hold on the board in the same hand as the gain. She told him he had not given an order to a person with a clipboard all evening and that he still has work to do.
- **Active want:** Keep the refuge locally controlled, keep the Quarter’s residents from being turned into infrastructure, and make sure the team knows that a blackout means the team goes dark.
- **Live problem:** Perry Dunne wants the crew gone by Friday, the Friday has not moved, and it is written on the board.

## Sable Arden

- **Professional position:** Havenport Continuity Office director. Holding a ninety-six-hour hold with no seal, no seizure, and a provisional classification, in exchange for the survey data in a sealed node and a supervised reading of the originals by Hollis Vane.
- **Emotional position:** Her fear of another mass casualty is specific and earned — Salt Crossing in 2019, six dead; Meridian in 2029, eleven dead; a 2031 contractor survey with a void migrating toward the clinic. She has conceded the 2035 holder-field loss in writing, has stated that her vehicle was in sector 4C at 05:02, and has said she does not forgive Elias for the door and intends to record what he got wrong. She is a losing administrator with a procedure, which makes her more dangerous, not less.
- **Active want:** Keep the classification provisional as long as possible, obtain the credential’s holder, and control what the review board sees first. Her 1800 digest answer is public, courteous, and built on eleven real deaths.

## Halla Dren — possible person, not a character in the present

- Nineteen years ahead, a shaft and outfall foreman. She gave no gift. She answered because the team had written down the right to stop before they went. Her want: the shaft is not named in any consolidated list, because a named location becomes one to be closed. Her reason: in her branch that shaft is the only air for about four hundred people. Her boundary: if the crew opens the shaft or moves anyone out of the Quarter without telling the people there first, she stops answering, and the changing branch makes warnings costly. **The team refused her request and recorded the refusal.** She was not asked a second question and must not be retried.
