# Character State After Chapter 40

## Elias Rook

- **External position:** Havenport Rescue Service field technician. **Stage 1: First Witness.** No active supernatural capacity and no summonable one. He holds the original notebook, an unsealed envelope he has now refused three times, and a public objection with his name at the bottom of it. He has refused, on the page, a route his sister offered him, and the refusal is his own and was made first.
- **Physical condition:** Left hand has persistent numbness and unreliable fine control, unchanged and unimproved. It is announced and the job is handed over, and in Chapter 39 it lay open on the table for twenty minutes while he argued himself out of a plan and nobody interrupted him. **In Chapter 40 he said out loud that he is not the person who asks his own future for anything**, and then accepted Joon's correction that the sentence means something smaller and truer. The hand is a limit, not a symbol.
- **The growth in this batch, precisely:** he refused a route that would have worked, **before anybody had finished arguing**, using Ansel Duarte's sentence on himself rather than having it used on him. That is the first time in the volume he has not had to be talked out of anything. **It is also the first time he has refused something nobody had accused him of wanting**, which is why it cost what it cost.
- **What he got wrong in this batch, on the page:** he wrote a three-stage retrieval plan with a date on it before dinner on Sunday, and called the first stage *access*. Nadi Bell made him say the word out loud. He also spent four days wanting the loading lip at four o'clock and wanting to tell a man at a wall about a road he had promised not to mention, and both of those are the same reflex. **He said *good* out loud and on the first try** when Sable refused to bind the Office to the hall's terms, and asked for it minuted, and said that four days earlier he would have written a page about why that sentence was the problem.
- **His four promises on the wall:** the five-signature promise (not below the west apron alone, not at all unless four of five agree in the same week, and if one says no he does not go and does not seek an overrule); *I am not going to be the last person to speak on any of this, and the last word does not close the item*; **Ansel Duarte may break any plan of Elias's by saying "that is a rescue," with no reason required and no requirement to be right**; and the new one, written by Petar Lisk: **he is not the person who asks his own future for anything.** He has broken none of them.
- **The envelope:** still unsent, and the reason has changed and is on the record. The road is available; the broker would carry a message for a fee; a message from him to his sister costs a night of somebody's feet in a ring on Calder Street. **The old reason was that he was afraid of a survey, and he has said the new one is better and that he does not like it better.**
- **Active want:** get to the old relay without a document, and find Group D, and learn who held `MNT-4C-17`.
- **Important limit:** he cannot summon a future voice, reopen the Still Hand, make a branch memory prove a route, ask Halla Dren anything, or ask the man behind the wall a second question. He has said all of that out loud in a room.

## Mara Okafor

- **Professional position:** Emergency physician and triage lead. Sole holder of the medical ledger, which the Office reads and never holds. Holds medical triage and the stop on moving any patient, and the Office's Friday medical standard is now a **present** standard with no borrowed capacity sought inside the entry window, written by her and attached to a chain.
- **Her three refusals, all standing:** **no medic on the line**; **no predicted outcome, no number and no category, anywhere in any document about that wall**; and, new in Chapter 36, **she will not write a rule — only a decision.** She wrote one decision with a time and her name on it, and said out loud with Osei eight feet away that she chose the nineteen-year-old's sacral lesion over the fusion patient's back, and that she would still have given the generator away at seven in the morning, because a seam over a school is worse than a back. **She said the hardest sentence first and did not soften it afterward, and Osei Kwarteng said *give it to the boy*, and nothing else was resolved.**
- **The argument she made in Chapter 40 that has to be carried forward:** she argued **for** using the Door Memory, out loud, about the unconfirmed reading at nine meters, and would not soften or withdraw it — and then argued against using a future person to authorize a present decision, on the ground that no present person may be required to authorize a future self's capacity and **the rule goes both ways and the both ways is the only part of it that means anything.** A future Mara answering for a present decision is not authorized by her, and a present Mara may not be.
- **Emotional position:** she has stopped being able to say no on his behalf and has started being able to say yes to something ugly and out loud. She refused the future self's gift as a route and refused to be comforted about the frame she gave away. In Chapter 38 she said she would like the audio to be a machine, which is the most honest sentence anyone in that room said all night.
- **Relationship with Elias:** professional, slow, built on conduct. She refused the good offer in Chapter 34 on the ground that the Office cannot be bound, and in Chapter 39 she told him the thing he said was the first thing she believed he would still be able to say in a month. **Nothing romantic has happened and no future version of either of them has promised anything to anybody.**
