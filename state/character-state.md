- **He has said four true things and given nothing anybody can hold.** The classification is not the whole cause and he will put the whole cause in his report if they can show him. A private record is not an alteration of a public one, and that is the sentence they have to live behind. An inquiry that rules on contested evidence becomes a trial. **And the part I can give you is small and I chose it.**
- **He was honest about the shape of his own theory**: a designation would have permitted entry, in the morning, into a corridor that terminates at eleven meters, and a licensed entry would have gone past the boundary and been blamed for it. **The Office's theory is smaller after day forty-two and not refuted.**
- **He read the fourth column aloud in a hearing room, all twenty-eight lines, and he has been to the wall and could not find an author, and he says that is not a gap in the evidence, that is its shape.**
- **He is not permitted to ask what happened to a letter he signed.** *It will be read by somebody whose job is to read things. That person is not me. I will not know what they do with it, and in nineteen years I have never once found out whether a letter I signed was read or filed.*
- **He cannot tell the team what a system gains by having fewer contradictions in it, and he said so in a room and asked for it to be minuted.** That is the volume's live question and it is his to answer in October.
