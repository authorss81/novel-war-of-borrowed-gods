# Character State After Chapter 10

## Elias Rook

- **External position:** Havenport Rescue Service field technician; Stage 1: First Witness after the completed Kestrel Still Hand loan. No active supernatural capacity remains.
- **Physical condition:** Left hand has persistent numbness and unreliable fine control. He can work, rig, and observe, but must compensate with his right hand and team support. The branch-memory afterimage is brief and no longer connected to an active loan.
- **Emotional position:** He has stopped treating a promise as permission to control every route and has accepted one refusal without forcing it. The loss of the service door, the empty-Car-3 record, and the chance to follow Nell weigh on him; he has not recovered Nell or resolved the seam.
- **Active want:** Find out what Nell’s branch-crossed signal means without treating it as a retrieval order; identify who used `MNT-4C-17` before the collapse; help decide whether another Kestrel entry is safe.
- **Working change:** He discloses the offer, boundary, target, return, refusal, and known perceptual cost; delegates movement order, medical priorities, records, and local power. He still reaches for solitary command under pressure, so the change is practiced rather than complete.
- **Important limit:** He cannot summon a future voice, reopen the Still Hand, or make a branch memory prove a route. His next promise must be bounded and shared.

## Mara Okafor

- **Professional position:** Emergency physician and triage lead for the Kestrel survivors; co-owner of the medical ledger and the refuge’s clinical decisions.
- **Emotional position:** Disturbed that Elias accepted a capacity carrying her name before fully explaining the branch-memory effect, but willing to continue working with him because he corrected the record and accepted a refusal. She does not treat his promise as authority over her or future Mara.
- **Active want:** Make consent, triage, and refusal legible in emergency practice; keep the twenty-three survivors medically accounted for; prevent a future voice from being treated as a silent resource.
- **Working change:** She sets patient movement and oxygen priorities, publishes the consent distinction, and supports a disclosure rule. She is an equal partner, not a prize or magical resource.

## Nell Rook

- **Status:** Missing and not extracted. Her direct pre-collapse alarm and later present-day branch-crossed signal prove voice and agency, not location, safety, or desire to return.
- **Active want within the evidence:** Warn Elias that the north stair is a branch, preserve a route or record, and make people who are erased by official histories remain witnessable. Her exact present objective remains unknown.
- **Relationship pressure:** Elias wrote a reply to her signal, withheld sending it, and sealed it in an envelope marked for filing only with receiver confirmation. It promises to hear her without making her choice for him. No one, including Nell, has received it, and the next batches must not turn the signal into a convenient command or retrieval proof.

## Tomas Vale

- **Professional position:** Senior safety instructor and structural support lead; has accepted the need for shareable safety, but remains inclined to protect the crew through a single senior call.
- **Emotional position:** Wry recognition that Elias is not repeating Samuel’s exact mistake, yet he is unsettled by the Office’s procedure and by how easily a safety rule becomes a command. He brings a support plan to the next comparison of records.
- **Active want:** Keep re-entry physically survivable and make safety shareable rather than another obedience test.

## Joon Park

- **Professional position:** Dispatcher and record keeper; preserves raw audio, paper measurements, duplicate copies, the 23-person count, and the `MNT-4C-17` clue.
- **Emotional position:** No longer willing to let a clean report erase a person. He understands preserving uncertainty as public responsibility, not indecision.
- **Active want:** Keep the original evidence accessible to survivors and make the record dispute legible before the Office seals Kestrel.

## Inez Sato

- **Professional position:** Cinder Quarter Electrical Cooperative organizer and local power steward. She has supplied a prototype refuge with recorded resident consent, refusal, and withdrawal.
- **Emotional position:** Willing to help the rescue team, but unwilling to let central institutions treat local infrastructure, names, or batteries as expendable.
- **Active want:** Keep the refuge locally controlled and useful without turning it into an unlicensed central command or a new loan circuit.

## Sable Arden

- **Professional position:** Havenport Continuity Office director; pressing for a sealed site, a controlled re-entry, and an equipment-failure report.
- **Emotional position:** Her fear of another mass casualty is sincere, and her safety argument is partly earned. She is unsettled by the pre-filled form, the unexplained route entry, and the team’s refusal to surrender the only originals, but she has not been identified with a larger faction.
- **Active want:** Make the site measurable and containable. The phrase “one approved outcome” and the 05:24 timestamp are unresolved clues, not a resolved ideology.

## Nadi Bell and the rescued groups

- Nadi Bell is alive with six other Group C members, including Ansel, who cannot walk. She gave Joon a family contact and a pre-collapse shuttle number; the Bell family appears on paper but not in the current digital response list.
- The twenty-three survivors are receiving ordinary care at the Cinder Quarter refuge. They are not interchangeable statistics, and their contradictory memories remain evidence alongside the official records.
