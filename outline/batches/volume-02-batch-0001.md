# Volume 02 Batch 0001 Outline: The Notice

- **Batch number:** 0002-0001 — the first prose batch of Volume 02
- **Chapter range:** Chapters 51–60
- **Working title:** *The Notice*
- **House style:** US spelling, matching the bible and the whole manuscript — *neighborhood*, *neighbor*, *organize*, *recognize*, *center*, *color*, *license*, *meter*, *apologizing*, *traveling*, *rumor*, *labeled*, *favor*, *artifact*. A grep for the British forms must return zero at the end of the batch.
- **Batch goal:** Two instruments arrive at a ward on Ash Street that has been running for a month — a Notice of Inquiry that argues the crew's choices caused the ground to behave as it did, and an Order of Impoundment that is faster and does not require anybody to be right. In the same ten days, a fifty-eight-year-old man who came out of a chamber under Kestrel on the thirtieth day tries to get his own name into a system and cannot, because an equipment failure does not carry passengers; his wife has been at a family-reception desk in Cordell since the Saturday of the collapse — **thirty-eight days at the midpoint and forty-three at the end of the batch, and every chapter states the figure for its own day and never the batch's**; **the residents refuse the demand for the residents' file in their own column and pay for it in public**; and the inquiry's second phase names a set of papers in another city that the Office does not hold. **The batch ends with the answer one building over and a request slip in Cordell with no name on it.**
- **Expected length:** 10 chapters, 1,800–2,400 words per ordinary chapter, landing nearer **20,000–24,000**. **This batch must open by cutting, not by adding.** Volume 01's five batches measured **16,097 / 28,159 / 25,643 / 25,281 / 25,402** after review. **Batch 0002, Chapters 11–20, at 28,159 is the largest overrun in the volume and it is the one that has to be named here rather than left off the list** — a figure for it was dropped in an earlier draft of this line, which is the reason this instruction exists. The overage was concentrated in the chapters permitted to run long. Only two chapters here are permitted to run over: **55 (the midpoint) and 59 (the climax).** Every other chapter is held at or under 2,400 and a beat is cut, never added. A finished scene governs length.

**The main length risk in this batch is repetition, not shortage.** Volume 01's late batches repeatedly re-stated a rule in a second character's voice for emphasis. In this batch, **each rule is stated once and paid once.** If Mara says something about consent, nobody else paraphrases it later in the same batch. If Petar writes a line in capitals, the next chapter does not restate it in dialogue. Budget for the "someone outside the team is right" beat **once** (Chapter 56) and for the "what does the word mean" beat **once** (Chapter 59). The short, concrete, human material — Cliff's spelling, Della's paper, the wall in chalk, the man who is one hand — is where the words go.
