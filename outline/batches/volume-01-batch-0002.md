# Volume 01 Batch 0002 Outline: The Map Under Dispute

- **Batch number:** 0002
- **Chapter range:** Chapters 11–20
- **Working title:** *The Map Under Dispute*
- **Batch goal:** Protect the names and testimony of the twenty-three rescued people, compare the physical route with the Civic Spine index, and build enough honest evidence to decide whether a controlled re-entry is possible. The central conflict is not whether uncertainty feels frightening; it is who gets to decide which contradictory account may become the official one.
- **Expected length:** 10 chapters, roughly 1,700–2,400 words per ordinary chapter. This is a measured budget taken from Batch 0001's actual output (1,288–1,948 words per chapter) and the batch phase timeout, not an aspiration. A finished scene governs length; never pad and never cut a beat to reach a number.

## Batch shape

- **Beginning — Chapters 11–12:** Ordinary care and the first full comparison of paper, memory, audio, and index. The refuge is stabilized enough to argue in, and the contradictions become legible without being resolved.
- **Escalation — Chapters 13–14 and 16–17:** The maintenance credential, Sable's prepared form, a missing family row, and Tomas's private admission each make the record a civic injury rather than a puzzle. Evidence is duplicated, compared, and argued by people with different risks.
- **Batch midpoint — Chapters 15–16:** The local vote splits the plan and distributes authority, then the Bell row's consequence lands: a living relative remembers the family while the digital list still says nothing. The team refuses a broker's convenient offer and keeps the discrepancy documented.
- **Batch climax — Chapters 18–20:** A limited thermal survey that cannot reach the deeper signatures, the Office moving to seal the site, and a batch-ending decision rather than a victory speech.
- **Aftermath — carried into Chapters 21–30:** A prepared, distributed survey or an explicit hold on the site, with separate custody of every record and a live question about who used `MNT-4C-17` before the collapse.

## Starting condition

- **Date and place:** 2042, the day after the Kestrel extraction, at the Cinder Quarter refuge and the surface triage area west of Kestrel Station.
- **Elias:** Stage 1: First Witness. The Still Hand is complete and unavailable, his left hand has lost sensation and fine control, and he has a habit of carrying decisions alone that he has begun to disclose but not solved.
- **The survivors:** Twenty-three people with paper records and a Civic Spine report claiming Car 3 was empty. Group C was behind the wall, not in the carriage, and that distinction must stay exact.
- **The record:** Paper manifest, raw relay audio, route measurements, and the Civic Spine index disagree. The service door is structurally unusable and out of the immediate index; physical absence is not proven.
- **The Office:** Sable Arden wants a sealed site, a consolidated list, and a controlled re-entry. Her safety argument is sincere and her 05:24 form is already written.

## Continuity bookkeeping for the batch

- **Group A:** Fifteen people from Car 3, including Tarin Voss, Niko Senn, and Anja Ro. Already at the refuge. Do not renumber.
- **Group B:** Oren Pike, the manifest passenger missing from the first count, already at the refuge.
- **Group C:** Seven people from the service alcove, including Nadi Bell and the nonwalking Ansel, already at the refuge. They were never inside Car 3.
- **Group D:** Two deeper heat signatures, unknown in number, condition, and route. Not rescued in Batch 0001 and not rescued by the Chapter 18 survey.
- **Paper count:** Twenty-three, unchanged. Any chapter that moves a person must update Joon's paper record in the same scene.
- **The credential:** `MNT-4C-17` is an internal maintenance credential on the 05:17 pre-collapse route entry, not a Civic Spine author record. Its owner stays unknown through this batch.
- **The clock:** 05:17 route entry, 05:24 equipment-failure form, 05:40 first rail alarm, 05:42 door observation, 06:27 rescue report. The team may compare these and may not convert them into a culprit.

## Future-voice ledger

- **No new loan is accepted in this batch.** The first Still Hand exchange is complete and cannot be reopened, extended, or retried. A refusal is never treated as a malfunction and is never retried as a shortcut.
- **If a possible voice answers in Chapter 18,** it must carry a branch-specific want, a reason for answering, a boundary that can be violated, and a present consequence of refusing. It must not be future Mara repeating the Chapter 9 one-body refusal, and it must not be the possible-Elias voice, who remains unidentified and offers no power.
- **The possible-Elias voice** may appear only as preserved raw evidence. It is not Elias Forty-Seven and is not identified as the Returning Hand in Volume 01.

## Interface and loan limits

- At most one short interface notice per chapter, only where the scene needs it, in plain language and using the approved terminology headings.
- Chapter 13 may not stage a second discovery of `MNT-4C-17`; the code and the question written under both signatures already exist.
- Chapter 16 may not re-stage the Chapter 9 handover of Nadi's contact details or the discovery of the missing digital row.
- Chapter 18 may not repeat future Mara's Chapter 9 refusal.

## Batch midpoint

Chapter 15 distributes the plan. The local vote approves only a limited survey or support role, with a withdrawal rule and no borrowed circuit, and a minority refuses. Authority is split by domain: Mara holds medical triage, Tomas structural limits, Joon records, Inez local power, and Elias physical movement without sole command.

## Batch climax

Chapters 19–20. The Office moves to seal Kestrel and seize equipment, and the team answers with a published disagreement, a bounded temporary hold or supervised document exchange, and separate custody of every record. Chapter 20 is a decision, not a victory speech.

## Aftermath

The team ends the batch with either a prepared, distributed survey or an explicit decision to hold the site while seeking a safer method. Nell's signal is still incomplete, the service door's physical status is still ambiguous, the possible-Elias voice is still unidentified, the Office's single approved outcome is a concrete institutional pressure, and the deeper signatures are still unaccounted for.

## Required character changes

- **Elias:** Continues practicing disclosure, delegation, and smaller promises. His left hand remains a practical limit rather than a symbol. He must let another person's refusal or medical judgment change the plan.
- **Mara:** Keeps present consent distinct from future consent, holds medical triage authority, and challenges unsafe re-entry language. Elias and Mara remain professional partners in a slow trust, not a romance.
- **Tomas:** Reveals that the 2034 Kestrel collapse involved the same old relay or its paired maintenance system, loses some unilateral authority, and offers a support plan that can be refused.
- **Joon:** Preserves both the credential and the uncertainty, and treats public record-keeping as a character choice rather than a technical job.
- **Inez:** Keeps local control, resident consent, and withdrawal visible. She is an ally with conditions, not a supply depot.
- **Sable:** Keeps a credible safety concern while showing how an early classification and a single approved outcome can exclude living people from the record.
- **Nadi and the survivors:** Receive individual choices, practical knowledge, and the right to refuse publication or extraction.

## Required power changes

None beyond recognition. Elias remains at Stage 1: First Witness, with no summonable capacity, no reopenable loan, and the ability to tell a future voice's warning or refusal from his own perception near an active seam. The refuge stays a physical and civic tool made of batteries, lamps, seam glass, analog maps, and barriers. Any apparent gain must be earned practice, a cost, or a changed decision, and a training gain counts only when it changes a choice or lets the team do a task that was previously impossible for a specific named reason.

## Required world reveals

- `MNT-4C-17` is an internal maintenance credential that predates the collapse, pointing at someone or some system that touched the route before the public alarm.
- Sable's equipment-failure classification was prepared before the public event, though not who prepared it or why.
- The Bell family's missing digital row affects a living relative who still remembers the family and the shuttle.
- Tomas's same-relay history supplies a practical reason to inspect the relay, and nothing about the Mercy Array's origin or the First Silence's cause.

## Open questions after the batch

- Who held the `MNT-4C-17` credential before the collapse, and who prepared the 05:24 form?
- What is behind the two deeper heat signatures, and can any of them be reached without reopening the altered route?
- What does Nell's branch-crossed signal actually ask for, and can her message be completed without making her a retrieval target?
- Who is the possible-Elias voice, and what does it want preserved?
- Can a re-entry be made safe under distributed authority rather than a single approved outcome?

## Chapter Cards

### Chapter 0011 — What the Survivors Need

- **POV:** Elias Rook, third-person close.
- **Location/time:** The Cinder Quarter refuge and its surface triage corner, the morning after the move from Kestrel.
- **Immediate goal:** Stabilize the first refuge and establish what each person needs before any re-entry decision is discussed.
- **Resistance:** Limited antibiotics, one oxygen supply, no beds, residents' privacy, the Office's demand for a consolidated list, and patients who do not want their names published. Care competes with record-keeping for the same hands and the same hours.
- **Information revealed:** The twenty-three are not a count. Nadi, Tarin, Oren, Ansel, and at least one Group A survivor each have a specific medical or practical need and a specific opinion about being recorded.
- **Action or decision:** Elias works the care line with Mara and hands decisions to people who own them. He records what he asks of the injured without turning the survey into an extraction.
- **Emotional change:** Elias discovers that the most useful thing he can do is not the most dramatic. Being needed for ordinary competence is harder for him to accept than being needed for a rescue.
- **Power or resource change:** None. Batteries, lamps, oxygen, and the seam-glass panels are the refuge's only advantages, and they are consumable.
- **Continuity fact to preserve:** Rescue and consent are separate. At least one survivor refuses public exposure while accepting care. The paper count stays twenty-three.
- **Ending type:** Practical beat / bounded plan.
- **Next-chapter pull:** Joon asks for the records to be laid out where everyone can see them, so the comparison happens in public.

### Chapter 0012 — The Map Under Dispute

- **POV:** Elias Rook, third-person close.
- **Location/time:** The refuge table, midday, with survivors, Joon's recorder, and the Civic Spine terminal within reach of each other.
- **Immediate goal:** Make the contradictions legible without forcing one explanation.
- **Resistance:** The terminal produces a clean route only by deleting evidence, survivors remember different corridors, and Elias's numb hand makes fine drawing unreliable. Two honest records cannot both fit in one index.
- **Information revealed:** The service door's unusability is measurable. Whether it shifted, closed, or became unsafe is not decidable from the refuge, and the team says so in writing.
- **Action or decision:** Elias draws the gaps in broad strokes and has Joon measure them instead. The team agrees on what is known, what is assumed, and what is missing.
- **Emotional change:** Elias gives up a precise map and gains a defensible list. He finds that handing over the drawing is not a loss of authority.
- **Power or resource change:** None. The refuge's analog maps are the only medium that can hold a contradiction.
- **Continuity fact to preserve:** Unsafe, shifted, and physically absent remain three different states. Chapter 25 owns the stronger absence proof.
- **Ending type:** Discovery / decision.
- **Next-chapter pull:** Before the Office seals the original logs, the team needs the pre-collapse maintenance route entry itself.

### Chapter 0013 — MNT-4C-17

- **POV:** Elias Rook, third-person close, with Tomas and Joon carrying the technical weight.
- **Location/time:** The refuge work area and the decommissioned relay point, late afternoon.
- **Immediate goal:** Learn what the second field on the pre-collapse route entry could have authorized.
- **Resistance:** Missing paper, copied credentials, a damaged relay, and Joon's fear of turning suspicion into a public accusation against a colleague.
- **Information revealed:** `MNT-4C-17` is an internal maintenance credential rather than a Civic Spine author record, and it predates the collapse. Tomas recognizes the format or its era without claiming to know the owner.
- **Action or decision:** The team compares the credential against the 05:17 entry, the 05:24 form, and the paper manifest, then copies the code into a duplicate ledger held separately from the originals.
- **Emotional change:** Elias is asked to sit still while others handle the evidence, which is harder for him than the investigation itself. Joon chooses accuracy over a clean accusation.
- **Power or resource change:** None. The gain is a second, independently held copy of a fact that can be lost.
- **Continuity fact to preserve:** The code does not identify Elias, Tomas, Sable, Nell, or any faction. The code and the written question are already established; this chapter investigates and does not re-reveal.
- **Ending type:** Bounded question.
- **Next-chapter pull:** Someone asks why an equipment-failure form was already filled in at 05:24.

### Chapter 0014 — The Form That Was Ready

- **POV:** Elias Rook, third-person close, with Mara controlling the medical ledger.
- **Location/time:** The surface triage area and a supervised document exchange point, following morning.
- **Immediate goal:** Obtain copies, witness statements, and a comparison the Office cannot dismiss as panic.
- **Resistance:** Sable controls access to some logs, Elias's refusal to surrender the original notebook looks like obstruction from the outside, and Mara insists the medical record carry the loan's exact boundary and return.

- **Action or decision:** Elias supplies duplicates under supervision, keeps the original notebook, and lets Mara sign a record that states the Still Hand's one-body boundary and wheel return exactly. Sable offers a controlled re-entry in exchange for access to the originals.
- **Emotional change:** Elias feels the cost of being an obstacle to a procedure that might be right. He refuses the trade without turning the refusal into a moral lecture.
- **Power or resource change:** None. What changes is custody: the team holds originals, the Office holds copies, and neither side can quietly rewrite the other.
- **Continuity fact to preserve:** `one approved outcome` remains an unexplained phrase on the form. Sable's safety concern stays credible and coercive, not cartoonish.
- **Ending type:** Costly offer declined.
- **Next-chapter pull:** Re-entry cannot be decided by the rescue crew alone, so the neighborhood has to be asked.

- **Ending type:** Reversal / distributed authority.

### Chapter 0016 — The Bell Row

- **POV:** Elias Rook, third-person close, with Nadi Bell as the person the chapter is actually about.
- **Location/time:** A borrowed phone line and a transit routing desk, then the refuge, late in the day.
- **Immediate goal:** Confirm that a living relative remembers Nadi and the shuttle without exposing her group to the Office or a broker.
- **Resistance:** Routed calls that pass through the Civic Spine, a relative who refuses to believe the official list, Nadi's refusal to have her name turned into proof of a larger theory, and a broker who is polite, useful, and dangerous.
- **Information revealed:** A missing digital row is a civic injury, not a puzzle. Someone with no record can still be a family member with a memory.
- **Action or decision:** The relative confirms the family and the pre-collapse shuttle number from memory. The team refuses the broker's workaround and keeps both records, Joon preserving the paper and the refusal.
- **Emotional change:** Elias sees Nadi refuse to be used as evidence and honors it, which costs him the cleaner argument he wanted. Nadi chooses her own family over his case.
- **Power or resource change:** None. The gain is a documented, living corroboration that the Office cannot dismiss as a person's recollection alone.
- **Continuity fact to preserve:** Nadi's contact, shuttle number, and the missing row are established canon. The broker is refused here and is not a new faction. The row stays missing and documented.
- **Ending type:** Costly confirmation.
- **Next-chapter pull:** Tomas asks for a private conversation about what he remembers from 2034.
