# Batch 0003 Summary — A Ward Made by Neighbors

- **Volume / chapters:** Volume 01, Chapters 21–30
- **Status:** Complete
- **Central beat:** Ten days in the Cinder Quarter, told as a sequence of rooms where the argument stops being about rescue and starts being about a neighbourhood's own calendar. The team takes delivery of the Office archivist's finding, builds a physical refuge out of scavenged material and ordinary money, puts a route nobody had documented back on the paper, makes contact with a man behind a wall, loses the first document that said a person was kept alive, and then loses the road itself. The batch ends on the volume's midpoint reversal — the **lower service corridor is gone**, from 31.0 m to 11.0 m, with residue at a place that has no place — followed by a slower distributed plan, a crew that thins, a broker who comes back, a promise that stays unsent, and a decision the team can actually keep: **the site is held.**
- **Length:** 24,946 words across ten chapters (1,856–3,200 each). Ordinary chapters sit inside or near the 1,800–2,400 target: Chapter 26 is 1,856, Chapter 27 is 1,947, Chapter 28 is 2,178, Chapter 30 is 2,104, Chapter 29 is 2,465, Chapter 23 is 2,574, Chapter 24 is 2,600, Chapter 21 is 2,835. Chapters 22 (3,183) and 25 (3,200) are over, and both are three-hander or four-hander scenes — a ward build with a gate, a review board and a procurement, and a reversal that cannot honestly be cut — that the prompt expressly allows to run long. ## What changed

- **The institution's own archivist wrote against his institution and in the room.** Chapter 21: **Hollis Vane** returns with four written findings because the hold said he would write them in the room. **Finding 1** — the equipment-failure classification is not supported by the Office's own evidence: the cause box arrives pre-ticked from the standing site pack, no field observation overwrote it, and no superseding cause was filed before the 06:27 rescue report. **Finding 2** — the file cannot be revised in any respect depending on credential attribution, because the 4C sector roster has no holder field, which makes the hole the only thing a board can act on. **Finding 4** — the classification must not lapse into the standing site default by silence. Sable rejects Finding 4 by name on Saturday as a request rather than a finding, and Vane writes back that she is entitled to say that and that he wrote it anyway.
- **Tarin Voss withdrew the strongest document in the building.** Finding 3 stated that a named subject keeps the right to withdraw the named field of a record without withdrawing the event. Tarin withdrew the field with his name in it — because *the body* is the word for a thing a loan gets used on — and Vane recorded the withdrawal **as a finding rather than as an absence**. **Niko Senn** withdrew a second page in the same way, because the note on it said *declined publication* instead of the wording he dictated. Two clean holes, made by the people the document was about. The withdrawal does not touch the offer, the boundary, the return, or the countersignature, which is what makes Chapter 25's proof possible.
- **The finding went on the wall with the Office's own answer underneath it**, in the same building, eleven inches apart, so the institution is on the record disagreeing with itself. The team asked the board **for the depot box, not for exoneration**, and lodged it before the board sat, which is how Chapter 24 happened.
- **Perry Dunne's date was Inez's negotiation, not Elias's.** The hold's last day is Saturday and the board sits Saturday afternoon with a team seat, so the crew cannot be absent. Perry's clean week moved to the following Friday. He said plainly that he did not move it to be helpful: *I moved nothing. You moved it.* His original date and the new one are both written on the board with his name against them.

- **The review board sat and gave and took in the same afternoon.** Granted: the citizens' request converted into a **survey condition** (joint inspection, resident observer, every measurement copied to the residents' file); the **05:02 dispatch original** to be produced to Vane in the hall; and the minutes to record Vane's Finding 4 wording. Withheld: no seal lifted, no hold extension, classification still provisional, the site reverting to the standing designation at midnight, and a condition with a date — a joint inspection **or a written statement of refusal, on the eleventh day, which is a Tuesday**. Sable's partial win and the team's partial win are both real and both on the minutes.
- **Contact happened, and it was not an extraction.** Chapter 23: six hours on the bore line, no medic on the line, Ansel Duarte's condition in force, and one **present-day** voice from behind the altered wall — not an echo, not a future, not a gift. No name, no count, no condition recorded, because none was given. During contact the terminal displayed a recommended re-entry and `Aftermath ahead: route will not survive another opening`, and nobody acted on it. The three wants: **keep the run on the paper, nobody comes in, do not write me down.** The reason he answered: the team published its own objection, so there was a record on his side of the concrete that could be argued with. Halla Dren was not asked anything and the log says so.
- **The archive gave a credential instead of a name.** Chapter 24: under the board's order, lawfully and awkwardly, with the box out at 11:04 and back at 11:41 on two cameras. **The second signature on the 05:02 dispatch sheet is not a name — it is `MNT-4C-17`**, written in a box that asks for a person. The **aisle log** shows box 214 opened on the day of the collapse at **14:12** under *Rout. arch. rev.* with the **subject field blank** — a field that exists, which is different from a field that does not. Joon stopped wanting Hoyt to like him and started wanting to be accurate. ## New canon

- **Hollis Vane's four findings** and the exact wording of each. Finding 1 (classification unsupported by the Office's own evidence), Finding 2 (the file cannot be revised while the holder field is empty; the 2035 roster could be indexed and has not been, in seven years, because nobody asked with a date on it), Finding 3 (a named subject may withdraw the named field of a record without withdrawing the event, and the withdrawal is entered as a finding and not as an absence), Finding 4 (the classification must not become the standing site default by silence).
- **A named subject may withdraw the named field of a record.** Established in the Office's own file and honoured. Tarin Voss and Niko Senn both used it. The medical page's offer, boundary, return and countersignature survive without a name.
- **`MNT-4C-17` appears as a signature.** The second signature line of the 05:02 dispatch sheet original is the credential string, not a person. A class-three maintenance credential cannot lawfully close a two-person Continuity inspection. The 05:02 sign-out and the 05:17 route entry are the same credential in the same morning; the holder is still unknown.
- **Box 214, aisle nine, Spine depot archive floor**, was opened on the day of the collapse at **14:12** under a routine archive-review entry with the **subject field blank**. A blank field is not a hole; it is a place where a name can later be written by somebody who was not there, without anybody lying.
- **Brauer's scan of the aisle ledger is missing its fourth column.** A single-pass scan of a bound single-sided ledger. The Office's copy of the log therefore has no subject column at all, and no one in that building can see the absence. Brauer will redo it on **Tuesday**, the eleventh day.
- **The lower service corridor is 11.0 meters.** It was 31.0 m on Saturday, laser-measured, photographed, keeper-signed, Office-observed, and walked again on Monday. The far twenty meters are not blocked and are not unsafe; they are not there, with residue at the boundary. The route index has no entry and reports a first recorded instance of 12:31 that week.
- **The corridor's usable span and the loan window coincide, and cause is not provable.** The honest finding is that the run's load-bearing span has only ever been documented inside the Still Hand window, and that no version of the opening rescue exists without the loan.
- **A present-day man behind the altered wall has been there eleven days and uses the lower service run.** He gave no name, no count, and no condition. He asked for three things: keep the run on the paper, send nobody in, write nothing more than *voice at the wall*. He stated a boundary with teeth — a body in the run closes the run — and a consequence: if the team goes in, the route shuts and people who are on no list stop crossing it.
- **Nell's fourth fragment: `do not use the west run`.** Four words, 02:04 Saturday, present voice, branch-crossed, on the 4C local route line, which runs through twenty meters that are no longer there. Joon recorded the carrier fact and refused to interpret the juxtaposition.
- **Three institutional patterns, named and unfixed:** a field that does not exist (the 4C holder, 2035); a field left blank (the aisle log, 14:12); a field never designed for the thing in question (the Office baseline survey, no keeper field, and a single *person present* line filled by the Office's own person).
- **The possible-Elias audio copy in the sealed node is 0.4 seconds longer than the team's**, with a flat carrier gap in the middle. Recorded as a fact. No interpretation, no name, no faction, and Joon has asked in public that nobody else put a meaning on it.
- **R. Adare has lodged a broker license application inside the Kestrel provisional classification.** The Office licenses brokers; a broker inside a classification is a licensing question, not a criminal one. The refusal is signed twice and the precedent is Nadi Bell's.
- **The Cinder Quarter refuge now has a dry end**: a block wall, a door, a lock, and a records room, built Monday out of nine pallets through the run. It is batteries, lamps, seam glass in a wooden frame, analog maps, and barriers on a feeder residents can cut. It is not a shared-load circuit and not a stage.
- **The Service recalled two of the six crew** for a gas main. Rafi and Kellan are gone; Doss covered the Friday nights. The hall lost its confined-space rope competence.
- **Ansel Duarte holds a break-power** over any plan of Elias's, and Elias signed it in those words.

## Proposed canon flags

- **The half-second audio discrepancy** (Chapter 30) is a new concrete artifact, not a new rule. It introduces no mechanic — it is a file-length difference with at least four ordinary explanations, and the text explicitly refuses to attribute it. Flagged because a later volume could accidentally treat it as evidence of institutional editing. If it is used again, it must stay a fact with no meaning attached unless a later batch deliberately earns the meaning.
- **The lower service corridor's geometry is now fixed:** loading lip at 0, dip at 9, anchor hole at ~10, termination at 11.0, inspection niche at 12 (gone), and the service door at 31.0 (unreachable, status unproven). Any later chapter that measures it must use these numbers.
- **The person behind the wall is a present-day civilian, not a future echo.** He must not be converted into a seam voice, a Branchread subject, or a source of a gift. He is the volume's first ordinary person on the other side of a wall, and his agency is a want, not a power.
- **The board's eleven-day condition** expires on **Tuesday** (the day after Chapter 30). Tuesday is also the day Brauer rescans the ledger and the day the shuttle confirmation is due. Three clocks land on one day and that congestion is available to the next batch.
