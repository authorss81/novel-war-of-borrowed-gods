# Batch 0001 Summary — The Ninety Seconds

- **Volume / chapters:** Volume 01, Chapters 1–10
- **Status:** Complete
- **Central beat:** Elias Rook’s ordinary Kestrel shift becomes a seam collapse. He accepts one bounded Still Hand loan from future Mara to keep Tarin Voss alive during present Mara’s repair, then loses the gift when the maintenance trolley’s third wheel stops. The repair changes a service-door route, and the door becomes structurally unusable and disappears from the immediate route index; physical absence, branch shift, and unsafe-but-present remain unresolved.
- **Rescue outcome:** Fifteen Car 3 survivors (Group A) and manifest passenger Oren Pike (Group B) reach the first refuge, producing a paper count of sixteen. Group C—seven people in the service alcove, including Nadi Bell and the nonwalking Ansel—reaches the refuge in Chapter 9, raising the paper count to twenty-three. The Civic Spine’s `CAR 3 PASSENGER LOAD: 0` report contradicts the physical Car 3 rescue; Group C was behind the changed wall and is a separate location.
- **Loan state:** Future Mara’s Still Hand was disclosed before acceptance, limited to Tarin’s one living body, bounded by ordinary treatment/repair and no killing or abandonment, and returned automatically at the third wheel. Elias has Stage 1: First Witness knowledge, persistent left-hand numbness/fine-control loss, and no active supernatural capacity. Chapter 9 contains a deliberate refusal of a second-body extension; it is not a new gift or a permanent prohibition on all future offers.
- **Signals and records:** Nell’s clipped signal is a present-day transmission carried across the Kestrel seam, not a future Nell echo or pre-collapse recording. An unidentified possible-Elias voice warns, through raw relay audio, against making the map agree; it offers no power or route and is not identified as the Returning Hand. The pre-collapse route entry bears an internal maintenance credential, `MNT-4C-17`; Sable’s equipment-failure form is timestamped 05:24, before the 05:40 first rail alarm and the 05:42 door observation. The deeper signal and code are not explained.
- **Aftermath:** Mara and Elias adopt a disclosure rule for every offer, boundary, refusal, and return condition. Inez’s Cinder Quarter cooperative creates a prototype refuge from batteries, lamps, seam glass, analog maps, and mechanical barriers, with resident consent and withdrawal recorded. Sable’s Office still wants a sealed site and a single approved outcome; Tomas, Mara, Joon, and Inez now have reasons to contest centralized control without abandoning safety.
- **Handoff:** Chapters 11–20 should investigate the route entry and `MNT-4C-17`, compare Sable’s timestamp with the pre-collapse record, reconcile the empty Car 3 report and Nadi’s missing digital family row, and argue over whether re-entry is safe. The first Still Hand loan is over; the possible-Elias voice and deeper heat signatures remain unresolved.
